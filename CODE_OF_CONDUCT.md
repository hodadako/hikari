# Code of Conduct

Lumina contributors are expected to make participation respectful, welcoming,
and free of harassment.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback,
and give others room to learn. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through
their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed with care and confidentiality.
